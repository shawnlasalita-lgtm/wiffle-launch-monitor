The app measures the wiffle ball's speed and launch angle in the first frames after impact, then flies a real golf ball with those numbers. If your real-ball carry is known, nudge the calibration until the sim matches.

Live camera watches the ball and fires on its own: tap the ball once, arm it, hit, read the result, drop the next ball on the same spot and it re-arms. Live mode needs this page open in Chrome or Safari over https. The clip buttons work from a video you recorded in your camera app.
